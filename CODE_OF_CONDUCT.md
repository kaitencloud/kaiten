# Kaiten Community Code of Conduct

Kaiten aims to maintain a professional, welcoming, and technically constructive
open-source community.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith;
- critique ideas and code without attacking people;
- welcome contributors with different levels of experience;
- keep discussions relevant and constructive;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries;
- accept maintainers' reasonable moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in Kaiten community spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory abuse;
- personal attacks or deliberately hostile conduct;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sexualized harassment or unwanted sexual attention;
- sustained disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation;
- knowingly exposing secrets, credentials, private data, or unpatched security
  vulnerabilities through public channels.

## Scope

This policy applies in Kaiten repositories and other official community spaces,
and when someone is representing Kaiten in an official community capacity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate moderation
steps when this policy is violated.

Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban from official
Kaiten community spaces.

## Reporting

For ordinary moderation issues, contact the maintainers through the relevant
repository or official Kaiten communication channel. To report a violation
privately, write to support@kaiten.sh.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately according to
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
