# Billing

The billing screens of the organization, where billing is on: the invoices it composed across its customers and instances, one invoice with the usage behind each of its metered lines, and the queue its accounting system reads. A finance person lists and filters the invoices, exports them, opens one to see what it bills and where it stands, settles it (marks it paid, writes it off, voids it, recomposes it, releases a hold), checks a metered line against the usage reports it came from, and follows what waits for the accounting system and acknowledges what was booked by hand. Where no payment provider collects an invoice, these screens are what make it auditable.

The screens of an instance's subscription, of a customer and of the billing settings are not here: they belong to `instances`, `customers` and `settings`. What the screens of billing share (the gate, the scopes, money, statuses, the invoice table, the way a refusal is shown) is in [`app/src/domains/billing`](../../domains/billing/README.md).

## Routes

Every route is under `/billing`, which `app/src/routes/billing/route.tsx` guards: where `GET /billing/capabilities` says billing is not there, no route below it loads anything, and an explanation stands in place of the screen. `/billing/invoices` and `/billing/handoff` are entries of the Billing section of the side navigation.

| Path | Route file | What it renders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/billing` | `app/src/routes/billing/index.tsx` | Redirects to `/billing/invoices` |
| `/billing/invoices` | `app/src/routes/billing/invoices/index.tsx` | `InvoicesPageContent`: the filters, the list, the export. The filters are the search of the URL |
| `/billing/invoices/$invoiceId` | `app/src/routes/billing/invoices/$invoiceId/route.tsx` | The layout of one invoice: it reads the invoice, names its breadcrumb and shows `BillingRouteError` when the invoice cannot be read |
| `/billing/invoices/$invoiceId` (index) | `app/src/routes/billing/invoices/$invoiceId/index.tsx` | `InvoiceDetailPage` |
| `/billing/invoices/$invoiceId/lines/$lineId` | `app/src/routes/billing/invoices/$invoiceId/lines/$lineId.tsx` | `LineDrilldownPage`: the usage reports a metered line was measured from. A line that is not metered, or that the invoice does not have, is a page that does not exist |
| `/billing/handoff` | `app/src/routes/billing/handoff/index.tsx` | `HandoffPageContent`: what waits for the accounting system and what was acknowledged. `?status=ACKNOWLEDGED` shows the second |

None of the dialogs has a route of its own. The audited actions of an invoice and the acknowledgement of a handoff are confirmations that open from their page and keep their state in the component (see [dialog via route](../../../docs/03-patterns/dialog-via-route.md) for what does get a URL).

## Data

Reads, all REST, none retried (a refusal is shown, with a way to ask again) and none suspended where the page has states of its own:

- `invoicesQueryOptions(filters)`: `GET /invoices` as an infinite query under the generated key, 50 rows a page, by cursor. The filters are the API's and are sent as they are; the same query is the one the export is built from.
- `invoiceQueryOptions(invoiceId)`: `GET /invoices/{id}`. The layout route loads it, and both pages under it read it with `useSuspenseQuery`.
- `lineReportsQueryOptions(invoiceId, lineId)`: `GET /invoices/{id}/lines/{lineId}/reports` as an infinite query by `afterSeq`, 500 a page. The generated options do not page this operation, so the key is the generated one marked as a paged read, which keeps an invalidation of the operation reaching it.
- `handoffQueryOptions(status)`: `GET /billing/handoff`, 50 a page, oldest issue first. Reading it never leases an invoice. The part of the queue is the `?status=` of the URL, read with the schema the API generates for it (`readHandoffSearch`), and the default one leaves the bare path.

The loaders of the list, of the line and of the queue warm their first page with `prefetchInfiniteQuery`, which never throws, and the queries have `retryOnMount: false`: a refusal the loader met is the answer the page shows, once, with a Retry, instead of a second request behind it and a flash of the error.

Writes use the generated mutations (`markInvoicePaidMutation`, `writeOffInvoiceMutation`, `voidInvoiceMutation`, `recomposeInvoiceMutation`, `releaseInvoiceHoldMutation`, `ackHandoffMutation`) through `useInvoiceMutations` and `useAcknowledgeHandoff`. None is optimistic: the page shows the invoice as the API answered once it has, and a refusal is read from the failure by the dialog that asked. A success calls `invalidateInvoiceQueries` of `@/domains/billing`, which refreshes the invoice, the lists under any filter, the queue and the lists of every instance; a 409 refreshes them too, since it says the invoice is not what the page showed. The exports (`useExportInvoices`, `useExportLineReports`) are mutations without an invalidation: they read and change nothing, and save the file with `downloadBlob`. The file names are built by the console (`invoices-by-line-20271004T153000Z.csv`, `invoice-1f3a9c2e-line-2-usage-reports.csv`), since the API's `Content-Disposition` is not readable across the origins of the local stack.

## Behaviour

- **Gates.** Billing is on, or the explanation of the guard shows. Each action is offered only to a session whose token carries the scope the contract gives its operation (`useCanPerform`), and only where the status of the invoice allows it (`getInvoiceActions` of the domain). A session that may only read sees no action, and no Acknowledge button.
- **The list.** Server paged, so no column sorts and no filter runs on the rows loaded: a filter on a page would miss the rest. The filters (status, kind, provider, handoff status, overdue, held, customer and instance slugs, a boundary period and an issue period) are in the URL, each set as it is changed; what does not read as a filter is dropped, field by field. A period is two UTC days, the second being where the period ends. The provider is a filter only where Stripe is connected. "Load more" reads the next page with the same filters. Overdue is not a status of the API: it is derived, for an unpaid invoice that is collected by `SEND_INVOICE`, from its due date. A held draft says it is held, and why on hover and on focus. `MANUAL` reads "Ready to bill", never as a failure.
- **The export.** Three files from the filters of the screen, never the page that was read: a CSV with a row for each line, a CSV with a row for each invoice, and NDJSON with an invoice and its lines on each line (`granularity` is only the CSV's). The export pages by itself, so it takes neither a cursor nor a limit.
- **An invoice.** Its lines in the order the API composed them, each with its service period and the arithmetic in the API's own words; the totals are the invoice's own fields, and the console adds nothing up. A metered line says what it was measured from: for an overage the limits that applied, for any metered line the fingerprint of its reports (`Reports 41–45 · 5 rows · Σ 172,345`) and, for a session that may read them, the way to them. A base fee, an add-on and a discount show neither. Who it was composed for is shown as it was then (customer, instance, license), however they were renamed or deleted since. A held draft shows a banner: the check that failed, every meter that failed with what was expected and found, and what each way out does under the provider of the invoice. A void invoice and its replacement point to each other. Nothing a payment provider adds (hosted page, PDF, reconciliation, retrying a push) is shown for a NoOp invoice.
- **The actions.** By status: a held draft is released, recomposed or voided; an invoice ready to bill is marked paid, written off or voided; a push that failed can only be voided; a void invoice is recomposed once; a paid, written-off or replaced invoice is final, and a provider-owned invoice offers nothing yet. Releasing a hold, writing off and voiding ask for a reason of one to five hundred characters, and the confirmation stays disabled until there is one; marking paid asks for nothing, and takes a reference to the accounting system, a payment time (read as UTC, never in the future) and a note. A refusal is shown in the dialog, which stays open with what was typed: on its field when it is about one (a reference that is too long, a time in the future), above the buttons otherwise. The dialog also stays open when the refusal leaves the invoice with nothing to offer: a 409 says someone else paid it or voided it, the page reads it again and its actions go, and the reason is still where the person asked. Recomposing a held draft rewrites it in place; recomposing a void invoice gives a replacement, which the page opens. When the API says the invoice is not a held draft, the recompose leads to one confirmation that voids and recomposes with one reason. When it says the invoice was already replaced, the page opens the replacement; when it says the instance was deleted, the recompose is shown disabled with the reason from then on. A void invoice whose period starts before the usage the organization keeps (`usageHistoryRetentionMonths` of the capabilities) has its recompose disabled with the reason, since the API would give a replacement with no usage line and no error; a held draft is not held back, since its usage is spared.
- **The reports behind a line.** Grouped by the reset window they counted in, in the words the console has for how a report moved the counter (append, set), each window with the sum of what its reports moved the usage by (or the overage, for an overage line), floored at zero as the invoice floors it, next to the quantity the line was measured at. A report where the limit in force changed is marked in words, since it explains an overage that is not the usage minus the limit of the day. A window the next page continues has no sum until the page that ends it is read. The sums are of usage, never of money, and are an aid to check the line, not a result. "Load more" reads the reports after the last one read; the CSV has all of them. When the API says the reports are no longer kept (a 422 on the age of the line's period alone, a draft included), the page shows what the invoice kept: the fingerprint, with no export. A 403 names the scope; any other refusal shows its detail and a Retry.
- **The queue.** Two parts, what waits and what was acknowledged, each oldest issue first, with the status each invoice is in now, how many times a consumer took it and, while a lease is running, until when it holds it. Voiding or writing an invoice off leaves a handoff that was pending pending, so a void invoice can wait in the queue: its status says it in the table, and its page says the system that reads the queue will see it as void or written off. There is no way to claim an invoice: that is the consumer's, a job or the CLI, and the empty queue teaches `kaiten billing handoff claim`. A person acknowledges by hand an invoice they booked themselves, with an optional reference and no lease; the dialog warns when a consumer holds it. A refusal shows its detail and the queue is read again.
- **Refusals.** The API's `detail` is shown as written, with the trace id of a failure that is the server's, a banner that names a missing scope and how a token carries it, and "nothing was changed, you can try again" for a 503. A refusal that is final (a missing scope, a record that does not exist) offers no retry that would change nothing. An invoice the API does not know is a page that does not exist. A dialog that was refused takes the focus onto the refusal, since its confirmation was disabled while the API answered.

## Tests

- Unit and component tests (Vitest), next to the code:
  - `components/__tests__/`: the invoice pieces (`invoice-detail-parts`: the actions bar, the hold banner, the chain, the handoff block, the line detail with its scope gate, the summary), the order of the writes of the actions (`invoice-actions-flow`), the audited dialogs (`reason-dialog`, `invoice-dialogs`: mark paid and acknowledge), the reports of a line (`line-drilldown`), the handoff table, the export menu and the filters toolbar (`handoff-and-lists`), the pieces the lists share (`paged-list`), and the two lists as a person meets them, with their paging, their refusals and their empty states (`invoices-list`, `handoff-list`). `billing-test-support.tsx` is the support of those files and no test: the real English and French texts (`useBillingTexts`), a router over plain anchors, a client that does not retry, the rows of a list, a page of a list and a refusal as the API answers it, with its status and its problem.
  - `utils/__tests__/`: the usage windows, the filters of the URL and their chips, and where a refusal of the API is shown on a form (`place-refusal`).
  - `schemas/__tests__/`: the reason, the payment and the acknowledgement, and the search of the queue (`handoff-search`).
  - `queries/__tests__/`: the CSV of a line.
- Stories (`components/stories/`, run by `pnpm run test:stories`): the invoice detail pieces, the usage windows and the notice for reports no longer kept, the handoff table and its empty states, and the skeleton, the empty state and the foot of a paged list (`paged-list`). `app/src/domains/billing/components/stories/invoices-table.stories.tsx` shows the table of invoices.
- E2E specs, `app/e2e/app/billing/`: `billing.invoices-list.spec.ts` (rows, filters in the URL and on the wire, paging, states, export), `billing.invoice-detail.spec.ts`, `billing.drilldown.spec.ts`, `billing.invoice-actions.spec.ts`, `billing.handoff.spec.ts`, `billing.errors.spec.ts`, `billing.noop-variant.spec.ts`, `billing.french.spec.ts` (every screen read in French); the screens are also in `accessibility/accessibility.billing.spec.ts` and `mobile/mobile.billing.spec.ts`. The data is `invoice-fixtures.ts` and the scenarios of `billing.scenarios.ts`, behind the mocks of `app/src/e2e/msw/billing-invoice-handlers.ts`, which filter, page, change state and refuse as the API does. A spec that needs a session with other scopes signs in with `signInWithScopes` (`app/e2e/app/_support/session-scopes.ts`), and one that reads the console in French and then navigates starts it in French with `startInLanguage` (`app/e2e/app/_support/language.ts`). Against the real API, `app/e2e/stack/billing-console.stack.spec.ts` has the API compose the invoices of a renamed customer and checks the console against them (see [the stack suite](../../../e2e/README.md#authenticated-stack)).

## Public API

`index.ts` exports the four page components, the query options the routes load (`handoffQueryOptions`, `invoiceQueryOptions`, `invoicesQueryOptions`, `lineReportsQueryOptions`), `readInvoiceFilters` for the search of the list, `readHandoffSearch`, `handoffStatusOf` and `toHandoffSearch` for the one of the queue, and `getInvoiceTitle` for the breadcrumb. It is imported by the routes under `app/src/routes/billing/`, and by nothing else: see [Import rules](../../../docs/AI_CONTEXT.md#import-rules).
