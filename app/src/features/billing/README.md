# Billing

The billing screens of the organization, where billing is on: the invoices it composed across its customers and instances, one invoice with the usage behind each of its metered lines, and, as a view of that list, the queue its accounting system reads. A finance person lists, searches, filters and sorts the invoices, exports them, opens one to see what it bills and where it stands, settles it (marks it paid, writes it off, voids it, recomposes it, releases a hold), checks a metered line against the usage reports it came from, and follows what waits for the accounting system and acknowledges what was booked by hand. Where no payment provider collects an invoice, these screens are what make it auditable.

The screens of an instance's subscription, of a customer and of the billing settings are not here: they belong to `instances`, `customers` and `settings`. What the screens of billing share (the gate, the scopes, money, statuses, the invoice table, the way a refusal is shown) is in [`app/src/domains/billing`](../../domains/billing/README.md).

## Routes

Every route is under `/invoices`, an entry of the side navigation of its own, which `app/src/routes/invoices/route.tsx` guards: where `GET /billing/capabilities` says billing is not there, no route below it loads anything, and an explanation stands in place of the screen. The queue of the accounting system is not a route: its two parts, what waits and what was acknowledged, are two of the status views of the list (`?view=waiting`, `?view=acknowledged`).

| Path                                 | Route file                                             | What it renders                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `/invoices`                          | `app/src/routes/invoices/index.tsx`                    | `InvoicesPageContent`: the list of every invoice, with the search, the filters, the export; `?customerSlug=` and `?instanceSlug=` scope it to a customer or an instance, the only part of the URL the API applies, and `?status=` and `?handoffStatus=` open it on a filter. `?view=overdue` and `?view=held` narrow it to those invoices, and keep the scope and the filters. `?view=waiting` and `?view=acknowledged` show the handoff queue (what waits for the accounting system, and what it acknowledged), and are all those views' URL holds |
| `/invoices/$invoiceId`               | `app/src/routes/invoices/$invoiceId/route.tsx`         | The layout of one invoice: it reads the invoice, names its breadcrumb and shows `BillingRouteError` when the invoice cannot be read                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `/invoices/$invoiceId` (index)       | `app/src/routes/invoices/$invoiceId/index.tsx`         | `InvoiceDetailPage`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `/invoices/$invoiceId/lines/$lineId` | `app/src/routes/invoices/$invoiceId/lines/$lineId.tsx` | `LineDrilldownPage`: the usage reports a metered line was measured from. A line that is not metered, or that the invoice does not have, is a page that does not exist                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

The views of the list are reads of two operations, so the URL names the view (`?view=`) and each view reads its own part of the search (`readInvoicesSearch`): `all`, `overdue` and `held` read the scope and the filters a link starts the list on, and `waiting` and `acknowledged` read nothing else, since each is a part of the queue. A link of the queue carries nothing of the invoices, and moving to the queue starts it over. The route's loader always loads the invoices of the scope, which the tabs count, and the part of the queue in the last two (`readInvoicesLoaderDeps`), so that moving between All, Overdue and Held reloads nothing. A value that is not a view reads as the bare path; the earlier `?view=handoff`, `?queue=`, `?held=` and `?overdue=` are not read any more.

None of the dialogs has a route of its own. The audited actions of an invoice and the acknowledgement of a handoff are confirmations that open from their page and keep their state in the component (see [dialog via route](../../../docs/03-patterns/dialog-via-route.md) for what does get a URL).

## Data

Reads, all REST, none retried (a refusal is shown, with a way to ask again) and none suspended where the page has states of its own:

- `invoicesQueryOptions(scope)` (`@/domains/billing`): `GET /invoices` read whole, 200 rows a request, until the API says there is no more (`allInvoicesOptions` of `app/src/lib/api/all-pages-query-options.ts`, under the generated key of the operation, so an invalidation of the operation reaches it). The scope, a customer or an instance, is the API's and is sent as it is; the search, the filters, the sorting and the paging are the console's, on the rows it holds, like the other list pages.
- `invoiceQueryOptions(invoiceId)`: `GET /invoices/{id}`. The layout route loads it, and both pages under it read it with `useSuspenseQuery`.
- `lineReportsQueryOptions(invoiceId, lineId)`: `GET /invoices/{id}/lines/{lineId}/reports` as an infinite query by `afterSeq`, 500 a page. The generated options do not page this operation, so the key is the generated one marked as a paged read, which keeps an invalidation of the operation reaching it.
- `handoffQueryOptions(status)`: `GET /billing/handoff` read whole, 200 rows a request, oldest issue first (`allHandoffOptions` of `app/src/lib/api/all-pages-query-options.ts`, under the generated key of the operation, so that every action on an invoice refreshes it). Reading it never leases an invoice. The part of the queue is the view of the URL (`?view=waiting` is `PENDING`, `?view=acknowledged` is `ACKNOWLEDGED`, from `HANDOFF_VIEWS` in `invoices-search.schema.ts`), typed with the status the API generates for it. The route loads it only in those two views.

The loader of the list warms the whole list of the view the URL names, the invoices or the queue, with `ensureQueryData` and the page reads it with `useSuspenseQuery`, like the other list pages: a refusal, in any page of the walk, is the route's, which shows it with `BillingRouteError` and a Retry that loads the route again, and their queries have `retry: false` and `retryOnMount: false`, so that a refusal is the answer and not a second request behind it. The loader of the line warms its first page with `prefetchInfiniteQuery`, which never throws: the page has the states of a feed, and shows a refusal once, with a Retry, instead of a flash of the error.

Writes use the generated mutations (`markInvoicePaidMutation`, `writeOffInvoiceMutation`, `voidInvoiceMutation`, `recomposeInvoiceMutation`, `releaseInvoiceHoldMutation`, `retryInvoicePushMutation`, `syncInvoiceMutation`, `ackHandoffMutation`) through `useInvoiceMutations` and `useAcknowledgeHandoff`. None is optimistic: the page shows the invoice as the API answered once it has, and a refusal is read from the failure by the dialog that asked. A success calls `invalidateInvoiceQueries` of `@/domains/billing`, which refreshes the invoice, the lists under any filter, the queue and the lists of every instance; a 409 refreshes them too, since it says the invoice is not what the page showed. The exports (`useExportInvoices`, `useExportLineReports`) are mutations without an invalidation: they read and change nothing, and save the file with `downloadBlob`. The file names are built by the console (`invoices-by-line-20271004T153000Z.csv`, `invoice-1f3a9c2e-line-2-usage-reports.csv`), since the API's `Content-Disposition` is not readable across the origins of the local stack.

## Behaviour

- **Gates.** Billing is on, or the explanation of the guard shows. Each action is offered only to a session whose token carries the scope the contract gives its operation (`useCanPerform`), and only where the status of the invoice allows it (`getInvoiceActions` of the domain). A session that may only read sees no action, and no Acknowledge button.
- **The list.** A list page like the customers: a search, a Filter button with its chips, the export where the page actions go, and a table sorted and paged in the browser, ten rows a page, newest boundary first until a header is pressed. The console holds every invoice of the scope, so nothing is counted from a page and nothing is "loaded more". The search matches who an invoice is for (the name and the slug of the customer, the name and the slug of the instance) and the invoice itself by its identifier, a part of any of them, in any case. The filters are the status (several at once), the kind, the handoff status, the provider (once Stripe is connected, and for as long as any invoice is Stripe's), overdue, held, the day an invoice was issued and the day its service period starts; a day is a UTC day. They run on the rows in the browser. The URL holds the scope, `?customerSlug=` or `?instanceSlug=`, applied by the API because it matches the slug a customer or an instance has now as well as the one an invoice was composed under. It can also say where four filters start (`?held=true`, `?overdue=true`, `?status=PUSH_FAILED`, `?handoffStatus=PENDING`), which is how the health of billing in the settings leads to the invoices it counts (it uses the first three; what waits for the accounting system leads to the handoff view): the chips show from the start, what the person changes is not written back, taking the scope off keeps them, and clearing them shows every invoice. The scope is a chip beside the search (under it when the row is too narrow for both, so that the Filter button and the export stay on the page), drawn like the filters, and taking it off leaves the bare path; the filters stay where they were. A link of an older version that carries other filters opens the list with its scope and the four filters above, and drops the rest. Overdue is not a status of the API: it is derived, for an unpaid invoice that is collected by `SEND_INVOICE`, from its due date. A held draft says it is held, and why on hover and on focus. `MANUAL` reads "Ready to bill", never as a failure. A list with no row says why: nothing yet and where an invoice comes from, nothing for the customer or the instance of the scope and the way to every invoice, or no match for the filters and the way to clear them.
- **The export.** Three files, never the page that was shown: a CSV with a row for each line, a CSV with a row for each invoice, and NDJSON with an invoice and its lines on each line (`granularity` is only the CSV's). The API makes the file, so it takes the scope of the URL and every filter of the screen that it has too (status, kind, handoff status, provider, held when it asks for "true", the day of issue as the period it is), and it pages by itself, so it takes neither a cursor nor a limit. What the API has no filter for (the search, the start of a service period, a filter that excludes) is named in the menu, above the choices, and so is overdue: the API has a filter of that name, but it counts an invoice overdue past its due date whatever collects it and whether its payment failed, and the screen only one that `SEND_INVOICE` collects, so the file would hold invoices the screen does not show. A file that differs from the screen is never handed over silently.
- **The page of an invoice.** The header (the title, the status and provider badges, who it is for, the actions) and, under it, three figures like the strip of an instance stay in place, and the rest scrolls under them: the total as the API states it, with how many lines it comes from and, when vouchers discounted it, how much they took off (`discountTotal`); the due date, in UTC, with how far away it is or how long it has been overdue, in danger like the badge of the title, or, for a draft that was not issued, the word that says it was not, or, for an invoice that was paid, written off or voided, when that happened, the day and under it the time of day, since the state itself is the badge of the title; and the period its lines bill, with the kind of invoice it is. The strip has two columns up to `lg`, with the period across the second row, and sets the period a size below the other figures, wrapping it after its dash when it is still too long. A figure has one home: the cards under the strip do not repeat it, the total apart, which the lines state again under their amounts, and the due day of an invoice that ended, which the summary keeps since the strip then says when it ended. Then a held draft's banner, as an alert across the page; then one row of cards aligned at the top, each ending with its content, like the cards of an instance ([detail cards](../../../docs/03-patterns/detail-cards.md)): the summary, who the invoice was billed to and, where it is in the handoff queue, the handoff, three columns from `xl`, two where the invoice is in no queue and from `lg`, one below; and the lines across the page under the row, with the totals ending where the column of amounts does. The summary says the boundary, who collects the invoice, since when a draft is held, when it was issued and on which terms, the day an invoice that ended had fallen due, the invoice it replaces or was replaced by, why it was voided and how a hold was released. The handoff says where the invoice stands in the queue, with what the queue does with it as its description.
- **An invoice.** Its lines in the order the API composed them, each with its service period and the arithmetic in the API's own words; the totals are the invoice's own fields, and the console adds nothing up. A metered line says what it was measured from: for an overage the limits that applied, for any metered line the fingerprint of its reports (`Reports 41–45 · 5 rows · Σ 172,345`) and, for a session that may read them, the way to them. A base fee and an add-on show neither. A discount says how it was composed, from the members the API records on its line and never from its label: what it takes, which invoice of its redemption it is out of how many, and the lines it bears on (`InvoiceLineDiscount`, in the billing domain). Who it was composed for is shown as it was then (customer, instance, license), however they were renamed or deleted since. A held draft shows a banner: the check that failed, every meter that failed with what was expected and found, and what each way out does under the provider of the invoice. A void invoice and its replacement point to each other, by a row of their summary. Nothing a payment provider adds is shown for a NoOp invoice. For an invoice **Stripe** collects, the page also shows, from the provider record the API mirrors and with nothing worked out by the console:
  - **Alerts** above the cards, each absent when there is nothing to say: the push a person asked for (running, or still queued after two minutes), a draft Stripe holds for a person to finalize ("Awaiting finalization in Stripe", the review mode of the connector), the error Stripe answered to a push that failed with the attempt count and the next attempt, and a failed charge with the customer's next step (`authentication_required` is the customer confirming on the hosted page; a code the console has no words for is shown as Stripe coded it).
  - **The provider card**, beside the summary: the status in Stripe (draft, open, paid, uncollectible, void), the invoice number and the identifiers (invoice and customer), when it was pushed and last read, whether the amounts match, how Stripe collects (it sends the invoice, or charges the card on file), and two links that leave the console, "Hosted invoice" and "PDF". They open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, only when the address is https (`getSafeProviderUrl` of the billing domain), and are read from the API each time and kept nowhere. An invoice Stripe does not have yet says so.
  - **The reconciliation card**, across the page, once Stripe has compared the amounts: a match says so in a line; a mismatch shows the two totals (Kaiten's and Stripe's, excluding tax, or the subtotal less discounts where tax is included in the amounts), the lines whose amount differs, the lines either side has alone, the discounts Kaiten composed that Stripe applied with another amount or not at all, and the discounts Stripe applied that Kaiten never created, such as a coupon added in its dashboard. Nothing is offered to fix it: the invoice in Stripe is the one the customer pays.
- **The actions.** By status: a held draft is released, recomposed or voided; an invoice ready to bill is marked paid, written off or voided; a void invoice is recomposed once; a paid, written-off or replaced invoice is final. Where Stripe collects the invoice, a draft the queue has not pushed is pushed now (**Push now**), a push that failed is retried (**Retry push**), a draft Stripe holds for a person is finalized (**Finalize in Stripe**, the same request, which the API answers at once), and an invoice Stripe has created is read back from it (**Read from Stripe**); an invoice Stripe has accepted offers that and Void, and neither mark paid nor write off, since the payment is recorded there. A push only queues the invoice (the API answers 202 with it), so after one the page reads the invoice again every five seconds for two minutes and says it is waiting; it stops when the invoice is pushed, when Stripe holds the draft, or when the push failed again (one more attempt), says how it went in a toast, and after two minutes says the push is still queued, which the queue keeps trying by itself. A refusal of a push or of a read (a held invoice, Stripe unreachable) is a toast in the API's words; when Stripe cannot be reached the toast says nothing was changed and offers to ask again, and the invoice stays what it was. Voiding goes through Stripe first: when Stripe reports the invoice paid and Kaiten has not read the payment yet, the API refuses (`paid_at_provider`), and the dialog reads the invoice from Stripe at once, once, so that it reads as paid, and closes on a word that says it was not voided; if that read fails, the dialog stays with the refusal and a button to read it again. Releasing a hold, writing off and voiding ask for a reason of one to five hundred characters, and the confirmation stays disabled until there is one; marking paid asks for nothing, and takes a reference to the accounting system, a payment time (read as UTC, never in the future) and a note. A refusal is shown in the dialog, which stays open with what was typed: on its field when it is about one (a reference that is too long, a time in the future), above the buttons otherwise. The dialog also stays open when the refusal leaves the invoice with nothing to offer: a 409 says someone else paid it or voided it, the page reads it again and its actions go, and the reason is still where the person asked. Recomposing a held draft rewrites it in place; recomposing a void invoice gives a replacement, which the page opens. When the API says the invoice is not a held draft, the recompose leads to one confirmation that voids and recomposes with one reason. When it says the invoice was already replaced, the page opens the replacement; when it says the instance was deleted, the recompose is shown disabled with the reason from then on. A void invoice whose period starts before the usage the organization keeps (`usageHistoryRetentionMonths` of the capabilities) has its recompose disabled with the reason, since the API would give a replacement with no usage line and no error; a held draft is not held back, since its usage is spared.
- **The reports behind a line.** Grouped by the reset window they counted in, in the words the console has for how a report moved the counter (append, set), each window with the sum of what its reports moved the usage by (or the overage, for an overage line), floored at zero as the invoice floors it, next to the quantity the line was measured at. A report where the limit in force changed is marked in words, since it explains an overage that is not the usage minus the limit of the day. A window the next page continues has no sum until the page that ends it is read. The sums are of usage, never of money, and are an aid to check the line, not a result. "Load more" reads the reports after the last one read; the CSV has all of them. When the API says the reports are no longer kept (a 422 on the age of the line's period alone, a draft included), the page shows what the invoice kept: the fingerprint, with no export. A 403 names the scope; any other refusal shows its detail and a Retry.
- **The views.** The list of invoices has a row of status views above its toolbar, where the Customers and Instances tabs sit, each with its count: `All · Overdue · Held · Handoff · Acknowledged` (in French `Toutes · En retard · Bloquées · Transmission · Acquittées`). They are links of one route (`RouteTabs`, which tells them apart by the search of the URL and draws the count of a tab next to its label): the bare path is All, the others are `?view=overdue`, `?view=held`, `?view=waiting` and `?view=acknowledged`, so the view survives a reload and the back button leaves it. All, Overdue and Held read the list of invoices (`GET /invoices`) and keep the invoices that pass the view's test, the test of the filter of the same name (`isInvoiceOverdue`, `isInvoiceHeld`), in the browser; the Filter menu narrows them further, and the search and the export stay. The export reads the view as the filter it stands for: Held is sent to the API, and Overdue is named as one the file leaves out, as the Overdue filter is. The counts come from that one list, which the route loads in every view and the console holds whole: All, Overdue and Held count the invoices that pass the test, Waiting and Acknowledged count the invoices whose `handoffStatus` is `PENDING` and `ACKNOWLEDGED`, so counting costs no request and a view with nothing shows its 0 (`countInvoicesByView`). Waiting and Acknowledged are drawn only where the queue matters: the organization collects through NoOp, whose invoices wait in the queue its accounting system reads, and the session holds the scope to read it (`useHandoffViewAvailable`), once the capabilities and the token have been read; elsewhere the row is All, Overdue and Held, and a link to the queue still opens it, or shows the refusal of the API, which names the scope. The views of invoices keep the scope of the list (`?customerSlug=`) in their links; the queue is the organization's, so its links drop it, and the counts follow the scope of the page. The page keeps one title, Invoices, over every view; the subtitle says what the queue shows in its two views.
- **The queue.** The Handoff and Acknowledged views. Two parts, what waits and what was acknowledged, two of the tabs above the toolbar (the views, above), each a list like the invoices: a search over who an invoice is for, the invoice itself and the number the accounting system booked it under, a Filter menu (the status, the kind, overdue) with its chips, and a table sorted and paged in the browser, oldest issue first until a header is pressed. The console reads the whole part, so nothing is counted from a page or "loaded more", and neither the search nor the filters are in the URL; each part has a search of its own, which does not follow the person to the other tab. Each row says the status the invoice is in now, how many times a consumer took it and, while a lease is running, until when it holds it; for what was booked, under which number and when. Voiding or writing an invoice off leaves a handoff that was pending pending, so a void invoice can wait in the queue: its status says it in the table, and its page says the system that reads the queue will see it as void or written off. There is no way to claim an invoice: that is the consumer's, a job or the CLI, and the empty queue teaches `kaiten billing handoff claim`. A person acknowledges by hand an invoice they booked themselves, from the check of its row, with an optional reference and no lease; the dialog warns when a consumer holds it. A refusal shows its detail and the queue is read again; the search and the filters stay where they were.
- **Refusals.** The API's `detail` is shown as written, with the trace id of a failure that is the server's, a banner that names a missing scope and how a token carries it, and "nothing was changed, you can try again" for a 503. A refusal that is final (a missing scope, a record that does not exist) offers no retry that would change nothing. An invoice the API does not know is a page that does not exist. A dialog that was refused takes the focus onto the refusal, since its confirmation was disabled while the API answered.

Against the real API, `app/e2e/stack/billing-console.stack.spec.ts` has the API compose the invoices of a renamed customer and checks the console against them (see [the stack suite](../../../e2e/README.md#authenticated-stack)).

## Public API

`index.ts` exports the three page components (the list with its two views, one invoice, the usage behind a line), the query options the routes load (`handoffQueryOptions`, `invoiceQueryOptions`, `lineReportsQueryOptions`; the one of the list is in the domain), `readInvoicesSearch` for the search of the list, `readInvoicesLoaderDeps` for what its loader depends on and loads (the view decides which read), and `readInvoiceListSeed` for the navigation, and `getInvoiceTitle` for the breadcrumb. It is imported by the routes under `app/src/routes/invoices/`, and by nothing else: see [Import rules](../../../docs/AI_CONTEXT.md#import-rules).
